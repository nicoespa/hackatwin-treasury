# Experiments

Generated by `treasury experiments --data data/sim/base --out reports/experiments` with `n_paths=1000`, origins
`default_origins(2026-10-31, n=8, step_days=7, horizon=14)` and `config/policy.yaml`.

| file | content | seconds |
|---|---|---|
| ablation_pooled.csv | forecast scores of models (a)..(d-), pooled over partners and horizons 1..14 | 13.7 |
| ablation_by_partner.csv | the same per partner | 0.0 |
| factorial.csv | reference (fixed buffer) vs proposed ((d) + forecast policy) x (wire, stablecoin), at config values | 1.2 |
| frontier.csv | capital vs shortfall rate per rail: (d) at each alpha, the fixed buffer at each number of days | 4.9 |
| designed_to_lose.csv | (c) vs (d) on five identical segments, 4 origins | 3.0 |
| unknown_scenario.csv | (c) and (d) under an unseen card campaign and cohort wave, announced vs surprise | 17.9 |

Amounts are in the simulation's currency (ARS). Shortfall windows are partner-days: a window is short
when the realized balance goes below 0 in it, and its shortfall amount is `max(0, -min balance)`.
`capital_total_mean` is the time-weighted mean of every partner's non-negative available balance plus
the deposits in transit, plus the central reserve (0 here); `cost_total` charges it at
`cost_of_capital_annual` over the backtest days, plus rail fees and `cost_per_usd_rejected` per unit short.
